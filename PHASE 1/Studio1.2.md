Visual Prompt Studio 1.2 Album Cover

Create an acoustic folk music album cover that focuses on quiet wilderness landscapes, organic textures like weathered wood, soft natural lighting, and a peaceful sense of space. There should be a slightly deteriorated 2 floor white wooden plank house medium far away on the middle leftish part of the frame and the house facing forward, there should be an old dead and dry tree a few meters on the right side of the house tall grayish with a few branches hanging down. A diagonal light ray of sunshine should be coming from the top right corner just slightly above the tree not shining over the entire house, but the sunshine specifically shines over the porch focusing on it. In the porch there should be a man sitting down in a chair in the rightish front side, almost at the edge of the house’s door playing a guitar facing slightly down with a cowboy hat that covers half of his face. There are mountains in the valley far off in the background giving the impression this place is similar to a ranch. The grass at the front of the house is rather tall and dry looking like a field of reeds, showing that it hasn’t been properly mowed in a while. There should be a symmetrical balance with the natural and non-natural lines thought the frame. The hue color of the overall picture should be slightly dim. Add a title in a fancy white font on the lower part of the image ‘A million Springs’.
