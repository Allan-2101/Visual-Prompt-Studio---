Visual Prompt Studio 1.1 - The Sci-Fi Film Poster Challenge

Create an illustration of a promotional science fiction non-cartoonish film poster with a futuristic background. The background should have different designs and lines both organic and non-organic, trying to assimilate different alien races technologies all blended together with different colors and geometric shapes. Small flying futuristic cars should be flying at the top with assimilating wavy lines giving the impression of flight. The main male protagonist should be up close to the shot facing half of his body towards the audience, wearing a spacesuit that is heavily damaged. The protagonist is crawling out of a hole in the ground just barely standing up with his gun in his hand and should look slightly disorientated. The floor on which he stands along with the hole should look like rocky and tech terrain damaged from a battle. A handful of aliens with the characteristics of a pig, others with goo type characteristics, others with four arms, another tall and slender with different color should be small and away from the protagonist, giving the impression they’re far away and chasing after the protagonist with different weapon in their hands for futuristic and primitive. The art style should be realistic with the protagonist; the aliens should look realistic too and slightly terrifying. Change the title to ‘‘After Party’’.

Glossary:
-Lines are an element that's used to direct the attention of the viewer towards something specific and can help to also give detail towards something.
-Shapes are usually with trigonometric forms like squares, circles or triangles.
-Texture is the physical feeling of something weather it soft or rigid.
-Volume is the space something occupies.
-Contrast can be opposite forms of something like colors, can either be shapes, colors, etc, and thus highlight each other.
